Sarvada Shiksha Samiti 02 · The Ground Reality
Understanding the Landscape

Why North East Delhi Needs Partners Like You

North East Delhi is among India's most densely populated districts — over 30,000 people per square kilometre. In localities like Sadatpur and Karawal Nagar, families are predominantly daily-wage workers, small traders, and home-based artisans. Many children in these households are first-generation learners. Government schools in the area operate in three to four shifts due to extreme overcrowding, with some classes conducted in open courtyards due to a lack of rooms.

While Delhi's total education CSR spending over the last decade reached ₹2,487 Crore, the distribution has been starkly unequal. Affluent districts like New Delhi received ₹1,119 Crore and Central Delhi received ₹733 Crore. North East Delhi — home to over 22 lakh residents — received less than 0.8% of that pool. This is the funding desert where Sarvada operates, and precisely where your partnership creates maximum societal return.

Education as the Great Equalizer

For a child born in Sadatpur, access to a modern classroom, a working computer, or a qualified science teacher is not a given — it is a privilege. Sarvada has been that bridge for 54 years. With the right partners, we can widen that bridge from hundreds to thousands.

Sarvada Shiksha Samiti 03 · Our Heritage & Legacy
54 Years of Building Futures

From a Single Classroom to a Community Institution

In 1972, a group of educators and community leaders in Sadatpur established Sarvada Shiksha Samiti with a single goal: to bring quality education to a corner of Delhi that the system had overlooked. What began with one classroom, a handful of teachers, and a borrowed blackboard has grown into a CBSE-affiliated institution serving over 1,200 students across Nursery to Class X, with three dedicated academic wings — Nursery, Middle, and Secondary.

For 54 years, the school has operated on a philosophy of holistic development — nurturing not just academic performance, but discipline, creativity, physical fitness, and moral character. Our alumni have gone on to secure admissions in prestigious colleges across India and abroad, a testament to the foundation we build. The school's motto — "Discipline is the ladder of success" — is not a slogan; it is a daily practice upheld by every student, led by the Head Boy and Head Girl.

A Banyan Tree, Still Growing

Like the banyan tree that spreads its roots wide to shelter all beneath it, Sarvada has extended its reach across generations of families in Sadatpur, Karawal Nagar, and surrounding localities. But to grow further — to give our students the digital, scientific, and creative advantages that children in South Delhi or Gurgaon take for granted — we need to invest in modern infrastructure, technology, and faculty development. That is why we are reaching out today.

Sarvada Shiksha Samiti 05 · Partnership Pathways
Beyond Financial Contributions

Every Form of Support Creates Impact

We believe meaningful partnerships go far beyond cheques. Whether you are a healthcare provider, a technology company, an individual professional, or a community group — there is a way for you to contribute that matches your unique strengths. We welcome all forms of engagement:

Health & Wellness Camps

Healthcare providers can conduct annual health checkups, dental screenings, eye tests, and nutrition assessments for students. A single camp impacts 1,200+ children and provides families with medical records they otherwise cannot afford.

Industry Expertise & Workshops

Share your professional knowledge directly with students. Lead sessions on coding, graphic design, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, journalism, or any skill. Even a single Saturday workshop leaves a lasting imprint on children who lack industry exposure.

Mentorship & Career Guidance

Guide senior students through college applications, competitive exam preparation for JEE, NEET, or CLAT, career planning, and interview readiness. Help first-generation learners navigate systems their families have never encountered.

Personality & Life Skills Programmes

Partner with us to run structured modules on public speaking, leadership, emotional intelligence, digital citizenship, and financial awareness. These programmes fill critical gaps that textbooks alone cannot address.

In-Kind Resource Sponsorship

Donate working laptops, library books, lab equipment, sports gear, solar panels, water purifiers, or furniture. Every physical asset is tagged, catalogued, and maintained on campus with full accountability reports sent to the donor.

Community & Environmental Drives

Organise plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, or cultural exchange programmes on campus. Corporate teams, college volunteers, and neighbourhood groups can use our school as a platform for social impact — benefiting both students and the community.

Educational Tours & Field Exposure

Sponsor student visits to science centres, museums, factories, universities, or nature reserves. For children who have never left their neighbourhood, a single field trip can reshape their understanding of what is possible in the world.

Custom Sponsorship Programmes

Have an idea we have not listed? We are open to creative partnerships — from sponsoring a photography club to setting up a school radio station or running a weekend art programme. Propose it; we will build the framework together.
